Common questions

How do I invite a teammate? Open Workspace and generate an invite code; your teammate enters it on their Workspace screen.

How do I access the shared Directory? The Directory and Benchmarks are part of paid plans, managed from the Workspace screen.

Reminders aren't firing. Reminders are local notifications; make sure notifications are allowed for ReferralFit in iOS Settings.

How do I delete my account? Email us from your account address and we will remove your account and workspace data within 30 days.
