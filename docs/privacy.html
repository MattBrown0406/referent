ReferralFit Privacy Policy

Effective September 7, 2026 · Last updated September 7, 2026

ReferralFit ("the App") is a professional tool for addiction interventionists and their practice teams to manage referral relationships and find clinically appropriate treatment placements. It is published by Matt Brown, Freedom Interventions ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Who the App is for

ReferralFit is intended for licensed or certified professionals and their staff. It is not intended for patients, clients, families, or anyone under 18.

Information you provide

  • Account information. Your email address and a password, used to sign you in. Passwords are stored only as secure hashes.
  • Workspace data. Your practice's referral partners (program names, contacts, phone numbers, email addresses, levels of care, insurance networks), referral and contact history, follow-up reminders, and notes you enter.
  • Case files. Case titles, stage, de-identified client labels, family contact details you choose to enter, payment status, and documents you attach. The App instructs users to keep case data de-identified and not to enter protected health information; you are responsible for the content you enter.
  • Directory suggestions. If you propose a treatment program for the shared directory, the program's public business details are submitted for review.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage and diagnostics. Basic technical information needed to operate the service (for example, timestamps of syncs and errors). We do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • Device storage. The App caches your workspace data on your device so it works offline, and stores your sign-in session in the device's secure keychain.

Permissions

  • Photo library / camera — requested only when you attach a document to a case file. Attachments are uploaded to a private storage bucket accessible only to your workspace.
  • Notifications — used for local, on-device reminders (daily briefing, follow-up cadences, consult alerts). We do not send remote push notifications.

How we use information

To provide and secure the service; to sync your data across your devices and share it with teammates you invite to your workspace; to process subscriptions; and to compute aggregate, anonymized network statistics (see below). We do not sell personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

Shared directory and aggregate statistics

The shared directory contains business listings of treatment programs (not personal data about clients). Usage statistics shown on listings (for example, how many practices refer to a program) are aggregated across workspaces and are only disclosed when at least five distinct practices have contributed, so no individual practice, professional, or client can be identified. Treatment programs cannot pay for placement or ranking.

Service providers

  • Supabase — hosts the database, authentication, and file storage. Data is protected with row-level security scoped to your workspace.
  • RevenueCat and Apple — process in-app subscriptions. We receive subscription status, not your payment card details.
  • Square and PandaDoc (optional) — if you link a case to a Square invoice or PandaDoc contract, those providers process the payment or signature under their own privacy policies; we receive status updates only.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is retained while your account is active. You can delete partners, cases, documents, and referrals in the App at any time. To delete your account and all workspace data you own, email matt@freedominterventions.com from your account email; deletion is completed within 30 days. Aggregate statistics that no longer reference your data are not affected.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest by our hosting provider. Access to workspace data is enforced server-side by row-level security. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and you should use a strong, unique password.

Children

The App is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Your choices and rights

You may access, correct, export, or delete your information by using the App or contacting us. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable privacy law; we will honor verified requests.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above will change and material changes will be noted in the App or by email.
